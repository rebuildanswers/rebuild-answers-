Before you commit

Nobody starts on the retainer

The read comes first, every time

It's a one-off and it's designed to be one. You get the prompt set, the verbatim answers, the competitor breakdown and the misinformation report — and then you decide, with real data in front of you, whether the monthly work is worth it to you.

If the read comes back and you're already winning your prompt set, we'll tell you that and you should not hire us. It happens. It's a better outcome for both of us than a retainer you quietly resent.

The six-month minimum applies to the retainer only, and it exists for one reason: off-site work takes three to six months to show up in answers. A three-month retainer would end right before the part you paid for starts working. We'd rather say that at the start than bill you for a quarter and shrug.

Can the read be credited against the retainer?

Ask. It's a reasonable thing to want and we'd rather discuss it on a call than publish a policy we haven't thought through properly.

What if I just want the tooling?

Take it. Answer Layer is MIT licensed and free, and the method is published in full. You do not need us to run it. What you'd be paying for is someone who knows which of the forty findings actually matters, and who writes the replacement copy.

Do you work with agencies?

Yes, white-label. Same numbers, your name on the report. Mention it on the call.

What don't you do?

Link building, keyword rankings, content volume, and anything on the refusals list. We're also not a reseller for any third-party AI visibility dashboard.
