What each entry will contain

Published complete, or not published

Every reading is a full disclosure, because a share-of-answer number without its denominator is decoration:

Published with every entryWhy it's there
The complete prompt setShare of answer is share of that set. Hiding it makes the number unfalsifiable.
Set version and freeze dateSo a later reading can be compared honestly against this one.
Runs per promptThree. One run is noise and we'd rather show the fraction.
Engine and model queriedOne named engine. Never the phrase "AI search."
The citation cartelWhich domains the engines actually reached for — usually not the organic top ten.
Verbatim excerptsThe sentence itself. It does more work than any chart.

Why bother

Trend data on a frozen prompt set is something you cannot buy

A single reading tells you who's winning a category today. Four quarters of readings on the same frozen set tells you who's gaining, who's decaying, and roughly how long change takes to show up in an answer — which is the question every client asks in month two and nobody in this industry can currently answer with data.

That's why this page exists and why it starts small. It compounds or it's worthless, and the only way to find out is to start taking the measurements.

If your category is one you'd like measured, say so. We pick the early ones partly on demand and partly on where the answers look most obviously broken.
